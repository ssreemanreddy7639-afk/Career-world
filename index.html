Discover interests

About this project

CAREER WORLD

A student-friendly career exploration website designed to make profession information easier to discover. It is an educational guide, not an official recruitment, admissions or salary service.

For government jobs, university admissions and regulated professions, use the relevant official government, university or professional-body website to confirm the latest rules.
